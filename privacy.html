Privacy Policy

VidoBento Uploader · Last updated: October 23, 2026

VidoBento Uploader ("the app") is a personal desktop tool that uploads videos to YouTube channels owned by its user. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

When you connect a YouTube channel, Google asks you to grant the app access to your YouTube account. The app uses this access only to:

  • upload videos you created to your own channel;
  • set titles, descriptions, publish times and thumbnails for those videos;
  • read basic channel information (channel name and ID) to show which channel is connected.

How data is stored

OAuth tokens and channel information are stored only on your own computer. The app has no server of its own and does not send your Google data to the developer or to any third party.

Data sharing

The app does not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to anyone. It does not use Google user data for advertising, and it does not use it to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can also delete the locally stored tokens by disconnecting the channel in the app.
