VidoBento Uploader

A personal desktop tool for publishing short videos to my own channels.

What it does

VidoBento Uploader runs on my own computer. It prepares short videos and uploads them to YouTube channels that I own and manage, using the official YouTube Data API.

How it uses Google data

  • It asks for permission to upload videos and set thumbnails on the channel you sign in with.
  • It only acts on your own channel, and only after you approve access on Google's sign-in screen.
  • Access tokens are stored locally on your computer and are never sent to any other server.
